# SECTION 7: PHASE 2 — SYSTEMIC TRANSFORMATION (2028-2035)

## 7.1 Strategic Objective
**Deepen and scale the transition: 43% emissions cut by 2030; 30×30 achieved; SDG gaps halved; regenerative systems self-reinforcing.**

Phase 2 moves from emergency stabilization to **systemic transformation**. The Foundation is fully operational, GRFF at $5.5T capacity, RTPs certified for 150+ countries, Mission Hubs at full deployment. The focus shifts to **structural reform**, **technology maturation**, **behavioral normalization**, and **institutional embedding**.

## 7.3 Structural Reforms: Locking In the Transition

### 7.3.1 Legal & Regulatory Frameworks (Enacted 2028-2030)

| Reform | Description | Enforcement |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Climate-Nature Laws** | Binding national net-zero + 30×30 + restoration targets; just transition guarantees | Constitutional / Supreme Court enforceable |
| **Corporate Duty of Care** | Mandatory transition plans (Scope 1-3), nature due diligence, director liability | Company law; civil/criminal penalties |
| **Financial Alignment Act** | All regulated entities: Paris/nature-aligned portfolios; stress testing; disclosure | Central bank / supervisor powers |
| **Subsidy Elimination Law** | Automatic phase-out schedules; revenue redirected to transition fund | Budget law; IMF Article IV monitoring |
| **Carbon Pricing Floor** | National floor $75-100/tCO₂ rising to $150 by 2035; border adjustment | Tax law; WTO-compatible CBAM |
| **Circular Economy Law** | EPR mandatory; right-to-repair; digital passports; recycled content minimums | Product standards; customs |
| **Land & Ocean Governance** | Spatial planning binding; IPLC consent; 30×30 enforceable; high seas treaty ratified | Planning law; UNCLOS/BBNJ |
### 7.3.2 Institutional Embedding

| Institution | Transformation | Accountability |
|-------------|----------------|----------------|
| **Central Banks** | Dual mandate: price + planetary stability; green refinancing; NGFS standard | Parliamentary oversight; public reporting |
| **MDBs** | 100% Paris/nature-aligned; country platforms; risk transfer; local currency | Shareholder mandates; independent evaluation |
| **Trade System (WTO)** | Climate waiver (Art. XX); environmental goods agreement; carbon leakage rules | Dispute settlement; trade policy reviews |
