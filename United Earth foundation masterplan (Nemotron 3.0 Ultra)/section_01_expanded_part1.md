## 1.2.2 Financier — Global Regenerative Finance Facility (GRFF) - Expanded (Continued)
**How it works (Detailed)**:
- **Tier 1: Core Capital ($100B)** — Paid-in shares from developed countries (0.1% GNI), SDR rechanneling. AAA rated, first loss position. Provides credit enhancement for all other tiers.
- **Tier 2: Concessional Window ($200B)** — ODA reallocation, philanthropic, carbon market revenues. Grants, highly concessional loans (0.75%, 40-year), guarantees. First loss / mezzanine.
- **Tier 3: Commercial Window ($200B)** — MDBs, DFIs, pension funds, sovereign wealth, central banks. Senior loans, equity, green bonds, sustainability-linked bonds. Senior, market rate.
- **Tier 4: Catalytic Capital ($50B)** — Private foundations, high-net-worth, corporate. Venture/early-stage, guarantees, first-loss. High risk, high impact.
- **TOTAL PAID-IN: $550B** — Leverage 10:1 = $5.5T lending capacity.

**Capitalization Roadmap**:
- **2025 (Pre-launch)**: $50B paid-in, $500B lending — Coalition of Willing pledges; SDR $100B reallocation agreed
- **2026**: $150B paid-in, $1.5T lending — G20/G7 commitments; MDB hybrid capital
- **2027**: $300B paid-in, $3T lending — Constitutional Instrument entry into force; first bond issuance
- **2028 (Full ops)**: **$550B** paid-in, **$5.5T** lending — Capitalization Conference; credit rating achieved
- **2030**: $700B paid-in, $7T lending — Retained earnings + new contributors; Tier 4 scaled

**Financial Instruments Menu**:
- **Policy-Based Lending (PBL)**: Budget support for RTP implementation — $500B/yr
- **Project Finance**: Revenue-generating infrastructure — $1.5T/yr
- **Program-for-Results (PforR)**: Outcome-based disbursement — $300B/yr
- **Guarantees & Risk Insurance**: De-risk private investment — $2T exposure
- **Green/Blue/Sustainability Bonds**: Capital markets access — $1T outstanding
- **Carbon Market Credits (Art. 6.4)**: Results-based finance — 2 GtCO2/yr
- **Biodiversity Credits**: Nature-positive outcomes — $50B/yr
- **Debt-for-Nature/Climate/SDG Swaps**: Fiscal space + outcomes — $200B cumulative
