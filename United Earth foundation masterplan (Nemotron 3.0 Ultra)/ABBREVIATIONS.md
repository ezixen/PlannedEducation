## B

**BA** — *Bachelor of Arts* — Academic degree; referenced in education capacity building.

**BAA** — *Baseline Assessment and Analysis* — Initial evaluation for RTP development.

**BAC** — *Business-As-Usual Climate* — Reference scenario without policy intervention.

**BACT** — *Best Available Control Technology* — Standard for industrial emissions control.

**BAP** — *Biodiversity Action Plan* — National plan for biodiversity conservation under CBD.

**BAT** — *Best Available Techniques* — EU industrial emissions standard (IED Directive).

**BATNEEC** — *Best Available Techniques Not Entailing Excessive Cost* — Cost-effective BAT.

**BAU** — *Business As Usual* — Reference scenario without policy intervention; baseline for mitigation assessment.

**BBB** — *Build Back Better* — Post-disaster reconstruction framework for resilience.

**BBNJ** — *Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdiction* — UN treaty for high seas biodiversity (2023).

**BCR** — *Benefit-Cost Ratio* — Economic efficiency metric (benefits/costs); Foundation target 3:1 to 8:1.

**BDB** — *Baseline Database* — Reference data for MRV baseline setting.

**BDC** — *Baseline Development Case* — Counterfactual scenario for additionality assessment.

**BDF** — *Biodiversity Finance* — Financial flows for biodiversity conservation.

**BDNF** — *Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor* — Referenced in health/climate mental health nexus.

**BDP** — *Biodiversity Development Plan* — National plan for biodiversity mainstreaming.

**BECCS** — *Bioenergy with Carbon Capture and Storage* — Negative emissions technology; CDR method.

**BEV** — *Battery Electric Vehicle* — Fully electric vehicle; key transport decarbonization technology.

**BFS** — *Biodiversity Financing Strategy* — National strategy for biodiversity finance.

**BGS** — *British Geological Survey* — UK geological survey; critical minerals data source.

**BHD** — *Biodiversity Heritage Dataset* — Global biodiversity data compilation.

**BHKH** — *Biodiversity and Health Knowledge Hub* — Platform for biodiversity-health nexus.

**BHP** — *BHP Group* — Major mining company; critical minerals stakeholder.

**BIM** — *Building Information Modeling* — Digital building design/management tool.

**BIL** — *Bilateral* — Two-party agreement (e.g., bilateral climate finance).

**BIMSTEC** — *Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation* — Regional cooperation group.

**BIT** — *Bilateral Investment Treaty* — Investment protection agreement between two states.

**BIOFIN** — *Biodiversity Finance Initiative* — UNDP program for biodiversity finance planning.

**BIP** — *Biodiversity Implementation Plan* — National biodiversity strategy implementation.

**BIS** — *Bank for International Settlements* — Central bank coordination forum; hosts Regenerative Finance Hub.

**BITS** — *Bilateral Investment Treaty System* — Network of bilateral investment agreements.

**BLM** — *Bureau of Land Management* — US federal land management agency.

**BIM** — *Beneficial Interest Mechanism* — Financial structure for community ownership.

**BIN** — *Business Identification Number* — Unique identifier for corporate entities.

**BIO** — *Biological* — Prefix for biological/ecological indicators.

**BIPV** — *Building-Integrated Photovoltaics* — Solar PV integrated into building envelope.

**BIS** — *Biodiversity Information System* — Data platform for biodiversity monitoring.

**BIT** — *Binary Digit* — Basic unit of digital information.

**BIV** — *Biodiversity Impact Valuation* — Monetary valuation of biodiversity impacts.

**BKF** — *Bilateral Climate Finance* — Climate finance between two countries.

**BLM** — *Bureau of Land Management* — US federal land management agency.

**BMBF** — *Bundesministerium für Bildung und Forschung* — German Federal Ministry of Education and Research.

**BMU** — *Bundesministerium für Umwelt* — German Federal Ministry for Environment.

**BMVI** — *Bundesministerium für Verkehr und digitale Infrastruktur* — German Transport Ministry.

**BMZ** — *Bundesministerium für wirtschaftliche Zusammenarbeit und Entwicklung* — German Development Ministry.

**BNEF** — *Bloomberg New Energy Finance* — Leading clean energy research provider.

**BNG** — *Biodiversity Net Gain* — UK policy requiring development to enhance biodiversity.

**BNM** — *Biodiversity Net Metric* — Metric for measuring biodiversity outcomes.

**BO** — *Budget Optimization* — Financial planning for GRFF allocations.

**BOAD** — *Banque Ouest Africaine de Développement* — West African Development Bank.

**BOD** — *Biochemical Oxygen Demand* — Water quality indicator.

**BOE** — *Bank of England* — UK central bank; NGFS member.

**BOJ** — *Bank of Japan* — Japanese central bank; NGFS member.

**BOP** — *Balance of Payments* — National financial accounts; climate finance tracking.

**BOT** — *Build-Operate-Transfer* — Infrastructure concession model.

**BPA** — *Bisphenol A* — Chemical of concern; referenced in novel entities boundary.

**BPM** — *Business Process Management* — Operational management methodology.

**BPM** — *Beats Per Minute* — Heart rate; health/climate heat stress indicator.

**BPO** — *Business Process Outsourcing* — Outsourcing model for GRFF operations.

**BPS** — *Basis Points* — Financial unit (1/100th of 1%); used in carbon pricing.

**BPS** — *Biodiversity Priority Score* — Prioritization metric for conservation.

**BRICS** — *Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa* — Emerging economies bloc; GRFF engagement.

**BRICS+** — *BRICS Plus* — Expanded BRICS cooperation framework.

**BRN** — *Business Registration Number* — Corporate identifier for GRFF due diligence.

**BRRR** — *Build, Renovate, Retrofit, Replace* — Building decarbonization hierarchy.

**BRT** — *Bus Rapid Transit* — High-capacity bus system; urban transport decarbonization.

**BS** — *Bachelor of Science* — Academic degree; STEM capacity building.

**BS** — *British Standard* — UK technical standards; referenced in building codes.

**BSI** — *British Standards Institution* — UK national standards body.

**BSP** — *Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas* — Philippine central bank.

**BSR** — *Business for Social Responsibility* — Corporate sustainability network.

**BSS** — *Battery Storage System* — Grid-scale battery storage.

**BST** — *British Summer Time* — UK daylight saving time.

**BTF** — *Bilateral Trust Fund* — Bilateral climate finance mechanism.

**BTR** — *Biennial Transparency Report* — Paris Agreement reporting requirement (Art. 13).

**BTU** — *British Thermal Unit* — Energy unit (≈1.055 kJ); used in energy statistics.

**BUB** — *Biodiversity Trust Fund* — Dedicated fund for biodiversity conservation.

**BWC** — *Biological Weapons Convention* — Disarmament treaty; referenced in biosecurity.

**BWD** — *Biodiversity Working Document* — Draft biodiversity policy document.

**BWP** — *Biodiversity Work Programme* — CBD work programme.

**BWS** — *Baseline Water Stress* — WRI Aqueduct water risk indicator.

**BYD** — *Build Your Dreams* — Chinese EV/battery manufacturer; technology partner.
