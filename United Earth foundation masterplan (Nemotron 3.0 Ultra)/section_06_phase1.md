# SECTION 6: PHASE 1 — EMERGENCY STABILIZATION (2025-2027)

## 6.1 Strategic Objective
**Bend the emissions curve NOW; peak global GHG emissions before 2025; stop nature loss; launch the Foundation architecture; mobilize $2T/yr regenerative finance.**

This is the "make or break" three-year window. IPCC AR6 is unequivocal: **emissions must peak before 2025** for a 50% chance at 1.5°C. Every month of delay adds ~3 GtCO₂ to the cumulative budget.

## 6.5 Phase 1 Governance & Coordination Mechanisms

### 6.5.1 Monthly "Planetary Pulse" Dashboard
- **Real-time tracking**: Emissions (Climate TRACE), Deforestation (GFW), Renewables (IRENA), Finance (OECD/CPI)
- **Traffic light system**: Green/Amber/Red on each 2027 milestone
- **Public & political**: Presented to UNGA, G20, COP monthly

### 6.5.2 Quarterly "Course Correction" Summits
- **Participants**: Foundation Executive Board + UNFCCC/CBD Chairs + G20 Presidency + MDB Heads + Civil Society
- **Function**: Review Pulse; adjust GRFF allocations; trigger emergency protocols; political unblocking

### 6.5.3 Annual "Global Stocktake Lite" (Interim)
- **Aligned with UNFCCC GST cycle** but annual frequency
- **Focus**: Implementation gap (not just ambition gap)
- **Output**: Country-specific "implementation scores" → GRFF allocation adjustments

## 6.6 Risk Mitigation for Phase 1

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| **Geopolitical fracture** (US-China, Russia-West) | High | Critical | Coalition of Willing proceeds in parallel; UN track continues; "climate club" trade measures |
| **Fossil fuel lobby capture** | High | High | Transparency mandates; anti-corruption; citizen assemblies; legal liability |
| **Debt crisis blocks investment** | High | High | SDR reallocation; debt pauses; Brady-style restructuring; GRFF guarantees |
| **Technology supply chains** (critical minerals, PV, batteries) | Medium | High | Diversification; recycling; substitution; strategic reserves; IRMA standards |
| **Social backlash** (yellow vests, farmer protests) | Medium | High | Just transition funded first; revenue recycling; participatory design; communication |
| **Climate impacts accelerate** (tipping points) | Medium | Critical | Emergency protocols; adaptation finance front-loaded; loss & damage operational |
