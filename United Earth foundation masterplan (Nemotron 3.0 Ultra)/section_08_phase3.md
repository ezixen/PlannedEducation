# SECTION 8: PHASE 3 — REGENERATIVE MATURITY (2036-2050)

## 8.1 Strategic Objective
**Achieve net-zero CO₂ globally by 2050; nature-positive world; universal well-being within planetary boundaries; regenerative systems self-sustaining.**

Phase 3 is the **consolidation and maturity phase**. The transition is largely complete in energy, transport, buildings. The focus shifts to **hard-to-abate residuals**, **carbon dioxide removal (CDR) at scale**, **deep nature regeneration**, **circular economy maturity**, and **intergenerational equity**. The Foundation evolves from "transition manager" to **planetary stewardship institution**.

## 8.3 Residual Emissions & Carbon Dioxide Removal (CDR)

### 8.3.1 The Residual Challenge (2035-2050)

| Sector | 2035 Residual Emissions | 2050 Target | CDR Required |
|--------|------------------------|-------------|--------------|
| **Aviation** | ~0.5 GtCO₂ | Net-zero | 0.5 GtCO₂/yr (SAF + DACCS) |
| **Shipping** | ~0.3 GtCO₂ | Net-zero | 0.3 GtCO₂/yr (ammonia + DACCS) |
| **Agriculture (non-CO₂)** | ~3 GtCO₂e | -80% | 1-2 GtCO₂/yr (soil carbon + BECCS) |
| **Industrial Process** | ~1 GtCO₂ | Net-zero | 1 GtCO₂/yr (CCUS + DACCS) |
| **Waste** | ~0.5 GtCO₂e | -90% | 0.3 GtCO₂/yr |
| **TOTAL RESIDUAL** | **~5.3 GtCO₂e** | **Net-zero** | **~3-5 GtCO₂/yr CDR** |

### 8.3.2 CDR Portfolio (2050)

| CDR Method | 2035 Scale | 2050 Target | Cost Target | Co-benefits/Risks |
|------------|------------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Afforestation/Reforestation** | 3 GtCO₂/yr | **5 GtCO₂/yr** | $10-50/t | Biodiversity, water, livelihoods |
| **Soil Carbon (Regenerative Ag)** | 1 GtCO₂/yr | **3 GtCO₂/yr** | $0-20/t (profit) | Food security, resilience |
| **Blue Carbon (Mangroves, Seagrass)** | 0.1 GtCO₂/yr | **0.5 GtCO₂/yr** | $20-100/t | Coastal protection, fisheries |
| **BECCS** | 0.1 GtCO₂/yr | **2 GtCO₂/yr** | $50-150/t | Energy + CDR; land competition |
| **DACCS** | 0.01 GtCO₂/yr | **2 GtCO₂/yr** | $100-300/t | Scalable; energy intensive |
| **Enhanced Weathering** | Pilot | **1 GtCO₂/yr** | $50-200/t | Soil health; ocean alkalinity |
| **Biochar** | 0.1 GtCO₂/yr | **1 GtCO₂/yr** | $50-150/t | Soil health; circular |
| **Ocean Alkalinity / Iron Fertilization** | Research | **0.5-1 GtCO₂/yr** | $50-200/t | Ocean health; governance challenges |
| **TOTAL CDR CAPACITY** | **~4.3 GtCO₂/yr** | **~15 GtCO₂/yr** | **Weighted avg <$100/t** | **Portfolio approach; sustainability screened** |

**Governance**: All CDR subject to **Oxford Principles** + **Foundation CDR Standard** (additionality, permanence, social/ecological safeguards, MRV). No CDR substitutes for mitigation.
