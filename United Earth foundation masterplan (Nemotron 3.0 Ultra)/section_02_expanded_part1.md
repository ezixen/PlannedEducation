### 2.1.1 Remaining Carbon Budgets (IPCC AR6 Synthesis Report, 2023) - Expanded with Full Sources and Logic
| Temperature Limit | Probability | Remaining Budget from Jan 2024 (GtCO2) | Years at 2023 Emissions (36.8 GtCO2/yr) | Source | Logic/Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1.5°C** | 50% | **~250** | **~6.8 years** | IPCC AR6 SYR SPM Table SPM.1 | 50th percentile of TCRE distribution; 250 GtCO2 from 1.1.2024 |
| 1.5°C | 67% | ~140 | ~3.8 years | IPCC AR6 SYR SPM Table SPM.1 | 67th percentile; higher probability = smaller budget |
| **2.0°C** | 50% | **~1,150** | **~31 years** | IPCC AR6 SYR SPM Table SPM.1 | 50th percentile; larger budget for higher temperature |
| 2.0°C | 67% | ~800 | ~22 years | IPCC AR6 SYR SPM Table SPM.1 | 67th percentile |
 **Critical Implication**: To have a 50% chance at 1.5°C, **global CO2 emissions must peak immediately and reach net zero by ~2050**. Every year of delay reduces the budget by ~37 GtCO2.
**Logic/Reasoning**:
- **TCRE (Transient Climate Response to Cumulative Emissions)**: IPCC AR6 assesses TCRE as 1.65°C per 1000 GtCO2 (likely range 1.0-2.3). This linear relationship between cumulative CO2 and temperature is the basis for carbon budgets.
- **Non-CO2 contributions**: The budgets above account for non-CO2 forcing (methane, N2O, F-gases) assuming ambitious mitigation. If non-CO2 mitigation is weaker, CO2 budgets shrink.
- **Historical warming**: 1.1-1.3°C already observed (2014-2023 average), leaving 0.2-0.4°C to 1.5°C.
- **Zero CO2 vs Net Zero**: Budgets are for CO2 only; net-zero GHG requires earlier CO2 net-zero to compensate for residual non-CO2.
**Sources**:
- **Primary**: IPCC AR6 Synthesis Report (2023), Summary for Policymakers, Table SPM.1, Section 3.3
- **Supporting**: IPCC AR6 WGI Chapter 5 (Global Carbon and Other Biogeochemical Cycles and Feedbacks)
- **Supporting**: Global Carbon Budget 2023 (Friedlingstein et al., 2023, Earth System Science Data)
- **Supporting**: Rogelj et al. (2019) Nature - Estimating and tracking remaining carbon budgets
**Logic/Reasoning for Budget Numbers**:
- The 250 GtCO2 (50% chance) comes from: TCRE median 1.65°C/1000 GtCO2 × (1.5°C - 1.1°C historical) = 242 GtCO2, rounded to 250 with uncertainty.
- The 6.8 years at 36.8 GtCO2/yr assumes constant emissions; reality requires immediate decline.
- Uncertainty ranges: ±220 GtCO2 for 1.5°C (50%) due to TCRE uncertainty, non-CO2, and Earth system feedbacks.
**Implication for Foundation**: The Foundation must ensure RTPs are consistent with a 250 GtCO2 budget (50% chance) or 140 GtCO2 (67% chance). This requires global emissions to peak **immediately** and decline ~7%/year (2.6 GtCO2/yr) to reach net-zero CO2 by 2050.
