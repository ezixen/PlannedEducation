# SECTION 11: JUST TRANSITION & SOCIAL EQUITY FRAMEWORK

## 11.1 The Justice Imperative

**No transition without justice.** The Foundation embeds equity not as add-on but as **design constraint**: every policy, investment, and technology deployment must pass an equity screen. The cost of injustice is transition failure — political backlash, stranded communities, deepened inequality.

| Justice Dimension | Principle | Operationalization |
|-------------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Distributive** | Fair sharing of costs/benefits | Progressive carbon dividends; universal basic services; GRFF allocation formula |
| **Procedural** | Meaningful participation | FPIC; citizen assemblies; youth/IPLC voting rights in RTPs; union co-governance |
| **Recognitional** | Respect for diverse identities, knowledge, histories | Indigenous knowledge co-equal; gender-responsive; disability-inclusive; intergenerational |
| **Restorative** | Repair past harms | Reparations for climate damage; remediation of sacrifice zones; land return |
| **Intergenerational** | Rights of future generations | Legal standing; long-term liability; 7th generation test for all decisions |

## 11.3 Just Transition Policy Framework

### 11.3.1 Five-Pillar Package (Mandatory for all RTPs)

| Pillar | Policy Instruments | Finance |
|--------|-------------------|---------|
| **1. Income Security** | Wage insurance (80% for 24 months); pension bridging; universal basic services (health, transport, digital) | GRFF Just Transition Fund ($100B/yr); fossil subsidy redirect |
| **2. Reskilling & Redeployment** | Personal transition accounts ($15k/worker); guaranteed training place; apprenticeship 20M/yr; recognition of prior learning | GRFF Green Skills Window ($50B/yr); employer levy |
| **3. Regional Diversification** | Place-based investment (€150B EU model global); enterprise zones; community ownership; infrastructure | GRFF Regional Transformation Facility ($200B/yr); MDB co-finance |
| **4. Social Dialogue & Governance** | Tripartite transition councils (govt, unions, employers); worker representation on boards; community veto | GRFF capacity building ($5B/yr); ILO technical assistance |
| **5. Rights & Protections** | Labor standards (ILO); health & safety; migration rights; informal sector formalization; gender equity | Legal reform; GRFF conditionality; Equity Commission monitoring |

### 11.3.2 Just Transition Fund (GRFF Window)

| Window | Annual | Target | Instruments |
|--------|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Worker Support** | $50B | Displaced workers (20M by 2035) | Income support, retraining, relocation, pension |
| **Community Investment** | $100B | 500+ transition regions | Infrastructure, SMEs, cultural regeneration, health |
| **Enterprise Transition** | $30B | Fossil firms → green firms | Equity, conversion finance, employee ownership |
| **Regional Diversification** | $200B | Coal, oil, gas, auto regions | Place-based; 10-year programs; community governance |
| **TOTAL** | **$380B/yr** | **Phase 1-2** | **Grants + concessional + guarantees** |

| Requirement | Implementation | Enforcement |
|-------------|----------------|-------------|
| **Free, Prior, Informed Consent** | All Foundation-funded activities affecting IPLC lands/territories | Independent FPIC verification; project suspension if violated |
| **Land Titling & Recognition** | 100M ha by 2030; 500M ha by 2050 | GRFF dedicated window ($10B/yr); legal support |

| **Cultural Heritage** | Sacred sites; languages; intergenerational transmission | Cultural landscapes program ($2B/yr); UNESCO partnership |
