# SECTION 10: TECHNOLOGY & INNOVATION DEPLOYMENT PLAN

## 10.1 Innovation Strategy: Mission-Oriented, Open, Equitable

**Core Principles**:
- **Mission-oriented**: Aligned with 10 Mission Hubs; problem-pull not technology-push
- **Open by default**: Publicly funded IP → open licensing for developing countries; patent pools
- **Equitable access**: Technology transfer mandatory for Leader countries; local manufacturing priority
- **Portfolio approach**: Multiple bets per challenge; stage-gate funding; pivot capacity
- **Systems integration**: Digital twins, AI optimization, cross-sector coupling

## 10.5 Intellectual Property & Technology Transfer Framework

### 10.5.1 IP Principles (Foundation Constitution, Annex)

1. **Publicly funded IP → Open License** (CC-BY / Apache 2.0 equivalent) for all developing countries
2. **Patent Pools** for climate-critical technologies (batteries, electrolyzers, DAC, SAF, vaccines)
3. **Compulsory Licensing** trigger: if technology not deployed in developing country within 3 years of maturity
4. **Joint Venture Requirement** for Leader country firms receiving GRFF: 30% local equity, tech transfer clause
5. **Indigenous Knowledge Protection**: FPIC; benefit-sharing; defensive publication against biopiracy

### 10.5.2 Technology Transfer Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Scope | Volume Target (2030) |
|-----------|-------|---------------------|
| **Mission Hub Joint Labs** | Co-development; local adaptation | 100 joint labs |
| **Patent Pools (Medicines Patent Pool model)** | Batteries, H₂, DAC, SAF, AgTech | 500 patents pooled |
| **Open Source Hardware** | Solar, wind, battery, electrolyzer designs | 50 core designs |
| **South-South Tech Transfer** | China/India/Brazil → Africa/LAC/Asia | $20B/yr flows |
| **GRFF Technology Finance** | Concessional for local manufacturing | $50B/yr |

## 10.6 Innovation Metrics & Stage-Gate Process

| Stage | Criteria | GRFF Funding | Decision Gate |
|-------|----------|--------------|---------------|
| **Discovery (TRL 1-3)** | Scientific breakthrough; mission alignment | $1-10M (grants) | Science Panel |
| **Development (TRL 4-6)** | Prototype; cost pathway; sustainability screen | $10-100M (convertible grants) | Hub Steering Committee |
| **Demonstration (TRL 7-8)** | Pilot at scale; business model; local test | $100M-1B (concessional + guarantees) | Finance Board + Science Panel |
| **Deployment (TRL 9)** | Commercial; cost competitive; supply chain ready | $1B+ (commercial window + private) | Investment Committee |
| **Scale (Global)** | 1%+ global market; local manufacturing in 3+ regions | Self-sustaining | Annual review |
