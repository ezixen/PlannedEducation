## 2.1 The Physical Reality: Planetary Boundaries & Carbon Budgets

### 2.1.1 Remaining Carbon Budgets (IPCC AR6 Synthesis Report, 2023)

| Temperature Limit | Probability | Remaining Budget from Jan 2024 (GtCO₂) | Years at 2023 Emissions (36.8 GtCO₂/yr) |
|-------------------|-------------|----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| **1.5°C** | 50% | **~250** | **~6.8 years** |
| 1.5°C | 67% | ~140 | ~3.8 years |
| **2.0°C** | 50% | **~1,150** | **~31 years** |
| 2.0°C | 67% | ~800 | ~22 years |

**Critical Implication**: To have a 50% chance at 1.5°C, **global CO₂ emissions must peak immediately and reach net zero by ~2050**. Every year of delay reduces the budget by ~37 GtCO₂.

### 2.1.2 Non-CO₂ Greenhouse Gases (IPCC AR6 WGIII)

| Gas | 2019 Emissions (GtCO₂e) | Mitigation Potential 2030 (GtCO₂e/yr) | Key Measures |
|-----|-------------------------|--------------------------------------|--------------|
| **Methane (CH₄)** | 11.3 | **3.5-4.5** | Oil/gas leak repair, livestock management, rice cultivation, waste |
| **Nitrous Oxide (N₂O)** | 2.7 | **0.5-1.0** | Fertilizer efficiency, industrial processes |
| **F-gases (HFCs, PFCs, SF₆)** | 1.4 | **0.8-1.2** | Kigali Amendment phase-down, alternatives |

**Total non-CO₂ mitigation potential by 2030: 4.8-6.7 GtCO₂e/yr** — critical for near-term warming reduction.

## 2.2 The Legal Architecture: Binding & Voluntary Instruments

### 2.2.1 Paris Agreement (2015) — Legally Binding Treaty (195 Parties)

| Article | Core Obligation | Operational Relevance |
|---------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Art. 2** | Hold warming "well below 2°C" and "pursue efforts to limit to 1.5°C" | **North Star** for all planning |
| **Art. 4** | NDCs every 5 years; "highest possible ambition"; "progression" principle | Foundation for national RTPs |
| **Art. 6** | Cooperative approaches (6.2), Sustainable Development Mechanism (6.4), non-market (6.8) | **Carbon market architecture** for GRFF |
| **Art. 9** | Developed countries "shall provide financial resources"; $100B/year floor | **Legal basis for climate finance** |
| **Art. 13** | Enhanced Transparency Framework (ETF) — biennial reports, technical review | **MRV backbone** |
| **Art. 14** | Global Stocktake every 5 years (first: 2023 at COP28) | **Accountability cycle** |

**Key Gap**: NDCs are **nationally determined** — no enforcement mechanism for ambition or delivery. The Foundation adds the **coordination and finance layer** to close this gap.

### 2.2.2 Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (2022) — 196 Parties

| Element | Target | Deadline | Foundation Role |
|---------|--------|----------|-----------------|
| **2050 Vision** | "Living in harmony with nature" | 2050 | Strategic orientation |
| **2030 Mission** | Halt and reverse biodiversity loss | 2030 | Operational target |
| **4 Goals (A-D)** | Ecosystem integrity, species, genetic diversity, benefits sharing | 2050 | Outcome metrics |
| **23 Targets (1-23)** | Specific measurable actions | 2030 | **Direct implementation targets** |

**Critical Targets for Foundation**:
- **Target 1**: Spatial planning for all areas
- **Target 2**: 30% degraded ecosystems under restoration
- **Target 3**: **30×30** — 30% land/sea protected (ecologically representative, connected, equitable)
- **Target 19**: **$200B/year** total biodiversity finance; **$30B/year** international flows to developing countries
- **Target 15**: Business disclosure of biodiversity impacts/dependencies

### 2.2.3 UN Sustainable Development Goals (2015) — 193 Member States

| SDG Cluster | Goals | Financing Gap (Annual, Developing Countries) |
