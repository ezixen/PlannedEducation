# SECTION 12: POLITICAL FEASIBILITY & LIKELIHOOD ASSESSMENT

## 12.1 Assessment Framework: Realist Optimism

This plan assumes **it will work out** — but grounds that assumption in a clear-eyed analysis of political economy, power dynamics, and historical precedent. We assess likelihood not as prediction but as **design parameter**: where barriers are highest, we build strongest mechanisms.

| Likelihood Level | Definition | Planning Implication |
|------------------|------------|---------------------|
| **High (70-90%)** | Strong momentum; aligned interests; proven pathways | Scale existing mechanisms; remove bottlenecks |
| **Medium (40-70%)** | Contested; requires coalition-building; conditional on leadership | Build enabling conditions; create "no-regret" moves; design fallback |
| **Low (10-40%)** | Structural barriers; vested opposition; requires breakthrough | Pilot at smaller scale; build proof points; prepare disruptive options |
| **Transformative (<10% alone, >50% with crisis)** | Requires exogenous shock or paradigm shift | Design emergency protocols; build latent capacity; narrative preparation |

## 12.5 Scenario Analysis: Three Pathways to 2030

### 12.5.1 Scenario A: "Breakthrough" (Probability: 35%)
**Conditions**: US-EU-China climate club; G20 subsidy phase-out; SDR $650B; GRFF $550B capitalized; RTPs 150+ certified
**Outcomes**: Emissions -43%; 30×30 achieved; finance aligned; just transition funded
**Foundation Role**: Full operational; stewardship emerging

### 12.5.2 Scenario B: "Muddle Through" (Probability: 45%)
**Conditions**: Partial cooperation; Coalition of Willing leads; GRFF $300B; RTPs 80+; carbon price patchy
**Outcomes**: Emissions -25-30%; 30×30 partial; finance 60% aligned; just transition gaps
**Foundation Role**: Coalition coordinator; gap-filler; proof-of-concept for UN track

### 12.5.3 Scenario C: "Fragmentation" (Probability: 20%)
**Conditions**: Geopolitical fracture; trade wars; debt crises; GRFF <$100B; RTPs <40; backlash
**Outcomes**: Emissions plateau; 30×30 missed; finance misaligned; justice failures
**Foundation Role**: Emergency coordinator; litigation support; latent capacity for rebound

## 12.7 Historical Precedents: Why This Time Could Be Different

| Precedent | Scale | Success Factors | Lessons for Foundation |
|-----------|-------|-----------------|------------------------|
| **Montreal Protocol (1987)** | Ozone | Science consensus; industry alternatives; multilateral fund; trade measures | **Tech readiness + finance + trade teeth** |

| **Paris Agreement (2015)** | 195 Parties | Bottom-up + top-down; 5-year ratchet; transparency | **Flexibility + accountability + universal** |
| **EU Green Deal (2019-)** | €1T+ | Legal binding; taxonomy; just transition; CBAM | **Law + standards + justice + border measure** |

**Key Difference**: This plan **integrates all five** — science, finance, law, justice, trade — into one architecture. No predecessor did.

## 12.8 The "Coalition of the Willing" Fast-Track (Plan B → Plan A)

If UN track stalls, **40+ ambitious countries + EU + 100 cities + 500 corporations** launch **Foundation 1.0** as voluntary alliance (2025):

- **Legal Form**: International non-profit (Swiss/UN host) + treaty among willing
- **Governance**: Same Council/Board/Secretariat; observers welcome
- **Finance**: $50B pledged → $500B lending via MDB partnerships
- **RTPs**: Voluntary but certified; GRFF access conditional
- **Mission Hubs**: Operational immediately
- **Absorption**: UN track negotiates in parallel; merger by 2028 (UNGA resolution)

**This ensures momentum regardless of UN consensus.** The best way to get universal agreement is to demonstrate success without it.
