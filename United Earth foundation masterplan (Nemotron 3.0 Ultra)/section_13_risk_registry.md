# SECTION 13: RISK REGISTRY & MITIGATION STRATEGIES
## 13.1 Risk Management Philosophy
**Anticipate, Prepare, Adapt.** Every major risk identified has a pre-negotiated mitigation strategy, trigger points, and responsible actor. Risks are reviewed quarterly by the Foundation Risk Committee and annually by the Council.
| Risk Category | Assessment Method | Reporting |
| Strategic | Scenario analysis; war-gaming | Quarterly Council |
| Operational | KRI dashboards; audit | Monthly Board |
| Financial | Stress testing; VaR | Monthly Finance Board |
| Reputational | Media/social monitoring; stakeholder surveys | Continuous Comms |
| Existential | Tipping point science; red teaming | Annual Science Panel |

## 13.3 Cross-Cutting Risk Themes
### 13.3.1 The Polycrisis Cluster (Risks 1, 3, 6, 7, 18, 19)
Interconnection: Geopolitics -> debt -> fiscal space -> adaptation gap -> L&D -> instability -> more geopolitics
Integrated Response: 
- GRFF as counter-cyclical lender (Brady 2.0)
- SDR reallocation as automatic stabilizer
- L&D Fund with innovative finance (not aid-dependent)
- Climate club trade measures as backstop
### 13.3.2 The Justice Failure Cluster (Risks 2, 5, 15, 16, 17)
Interconnection: Capture -> exclusion -> backlash -> populism -> more capture
Integrated Response:
- Equity Commission veto power
- Just Transition Fund first-dollar principle
- FPIC as condition precedent
- Participatory governance (citizen assemblies, youth/IPLC veto)
### 13.3.3 The Implementation Gap Cluster (Risks 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14)
Interconnection: Tech risk -> market risk -> verification risk -> coordination risk -> data risk
Integrated Response:
- Stage-gate innovation with pivot triggers
- Unified MRV platform (Art. 6.4 + GBF + SDG)
- Mission Hub integration unit
- Science Panel early warning

## 13.5 Risk Appetite Statement
The Foundation accepts:
- High financial risk on catalytic investments (Tier 4) - expected loss rate 20-30%
- Medium reputational risk on bold policies (carbon pricing, subsidy reform) - necessary for impact
- Low regulatory risk - full compliance; conservative interpretation
The Foundation does NOT accept:
- Any risk to IPLC rights / FPIC - zero tolerance
- Any risk to worker livelihoods without transition - just transition first dollar
- Any risk of greenwashing in GRFF portfolio - strict additionality
- Any existential risk without pre-authorized protocol - planetary emergency, financial contagion
