# SECTION 4: FINANCE ARCHITECTURE — GLOBAL REGENERATIVE FINANCE FACILITY (GRFF)

## 4.1 The Finance Challenge: Orders of Magnitude

| Flow | Current Annual (2023-24) | Required Annual (2025-2030) | Gap | Source |
|------|--------------------------|------------------------------|-----|--------|
| **Climate Mitigation** | ~$1.3T | **$4.5-5.5T** | **$3.2-4.2T** | IEA NZE 2023, CPI |
| **Climate Adaptation** | ~$63B | **$300-400B** | **$240-340B** | UNEP Adaptation Gap 2023 |

**Key Insight**: The money exists — it's **misallocated**. Redirecting fossil fuel subsidies ($7T) + ending harmful subsidies ($1.8T agriculture/fisheries) + carbon pricing ($200-500B) + MDB balance sheet optimization ($500B) + SDR reallocation ($650B) + private capital mobilization (10:1 leverage) = **$10T+ available**.

## 4.5 Allocation Framework: Equity + Impact + Additionality

### 4.5.1 Allocation Formula (Weighted)

```
Country Allocation = Base × [Need Index × 0.4 + Impact Potential × 0.3 + Readiness × 0.2 + Equity Weight × 0.1]

Where:
- Need Index = f(vulnerability, poverty, emissions gap, biodiversity gap, SDG gap)
- Impact Potential = f(mitigation tCO₂/$, biodiversity ha/$, SDG beneficiaries/$)
- Readiness = f(RTP certification, pipeline quality, institutional capacity)
- Equity Weight = 2.0 for LDCs/SIDS, 1.5 for LMICs, 1.0 for UMICs, 0.5 for HICs
```
