# MIT 18.01: Single Variable Calculus

- **Institution**: Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
- **Department**: Mathematics (Course 18)
- **Instructor**: Prof. David Jerison
- **Level**: Undergraduate (General Institute Requirement)
- **Source**: MIT OpenCourseWare (https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-01sc-single-variable-calculus-fall-2010/)
- **Course Description**: Differentiation and integration of functions of one variable, with applications to optimization, graphing, area, volume, and differential equations.

---

## Course Syllabus & Core Units

### Unit 1: Differentiation
- Geometric interpretation of the derivative: Slope of tangent lines, rates of change.
- Formal definition: $\lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x + \Delta x) - f(x)}{\Delta x}$.
- Rules of differentiation: Product rule, quotient rule, chain rule.
- Implicit differentiation and inverse trigonometric functions.
- Higher derivatives and physical interpretations (velocity, acceleration, jerk).

### Unit 2: Applications of Differentiation
- Linear approximations, differential $df = f'(x) dx$, and error estimation.
- Curve sketching: First derivative test, critical points, concavity, second derivative test, inflection points.
- Mean Value Theorem and Rolle's Theorem.
- Optimization problems (maximizing/minimizing area, volume, cost).
- Related rates and dynamic geometric problems.
- Newton's method for root finding and convergence conditions.
- Indeterminate forms and L'Hôpital's Rule.

### Unit 3: The Definite Integral and Its Applications
- Riemann sums, area under curves, upper and lower sums.
- Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: Part 1 ($d/dx \int_a^x f(t)dt = f(x)$) and Part 2 ($\int_a^b f'(x)dx = f(b) - f(a)$).
- Applications of definite integrals:
  - Areas between curves.
  - Volumes of revolution (disk method, washer method, cylindrical shell method).
  - Arc length and surface area of revolution.
  - Work, center of mass, and average value of functions.

### Unit 4: Techniques of Integration
- Substitution method ($u$-substitution).
- Integration by parts: $\int u dv = uv - \int v du$.
- Trigonometric integrals and trigonometric substitutions ($x = a \sin \theta$, $x = a \tan \theta$, $x = a \sec \theta$).
- Partial fractions decomposition for rational functions.
- Improper integrals: Infinite limits and infinite integrands, convergence tests.

### Unit 5: Sequences, Series, and Taylor Approximations
- Sequences and limits of sequences.
- Infinite series, partial sums, and convergence tests (Integral test, Comparison test, Limit Comparison test, Alternating Series test, Ratio test).
- Power series and radius/interval of convergence.
- Taylor and Maclaurin series expansions: $f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!} (x - a)^n$.
- Taylor's Remainder Theorem and error bounds.
