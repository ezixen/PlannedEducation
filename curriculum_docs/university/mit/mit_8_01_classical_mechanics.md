## Course Syllabus & Core Units

### Unit 1: Kinematics in 1D and Multi-Dimensions
- Vectors, coordinate systems (Cartesian, polar, cylindrical).
- 1D Kinematics: Position, velocity, acceleration as derivatives and integrals.
- 2D/3D Kinematics: Projectile motion, relative velocity, Galilean transformations.
- Circular Motion: Angular velocity $\omega$, tangential acceleration, centripetal acceleration $a_c = v^2/r = \omega^2 r$.

### Unit 2: Newton's Laws of Motion
- Newton's First, Second, and Third Laws ($\vec{F}_{\text{net}} = m \vec{a}$, action-reaction pairs).
- Free body diagrams and force analysis.
- Friction forces: Static friction ($f_s \le \mu_s N$), kinetic friction ($f_k = \mu_k N$), drag forces.
- Tension, normal forces, spring forces (Hooke's Law: $F = -kx$).
- Non-inertial frames and fictitious forces (centrifugal, Coriolis).

### Unit 3: Work, Energy, and Conservation Laws
- Work done by constant and variable forces: $W = \int \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r}$.
- Work-Kinetic Energy Theorem: $W_{\text{net}} = \Delta K$.
- Conservative forces and potential energy functions: $\vec{F} = -\nabla U$.
- Conservation of mechanical energy: $E_{\text{mech}} = K + U = \text{constant}$.
- Power: $P = dW/dt = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}$.

### Unit 4: Momentum, Impulse, and Collisions
- Linear momentum: $\vec{p} = m \vec{v}$.
- Impulse and impulse-momentum theorem: $\vec{J} = \int \vec{F} dt = \Delta \vec{p}$.
- System of particles and center of mass: $\vec{R}_{\text{cm}} = \frac{1}{M}\sum m_i \vec{r}_i$.
- Conservation of linear momentum in closed systems.
- Elastic vs. inelastic collisions in 1D and 2D. Rocket propulsion and variable mass systems.

### Unit 5: Rotational Motion and Angular Momentum
- Torque: $\vec{\tau} = \vec{r} \times \vec{F}$.
- Moment of inertia: $I = \int r^2 dm$, Parallel-Axis Theorem ($I = I_{\text{cm}} + M d^2$).
- Rotational dynamics: $\vec{\tau}_{\text{net}} = I \vec{\alpha} = d\vec{L}/dt$.
- Angular momentum: $\vec{L} = \vec{r} \times \vec{p} = I \vec{\omega}$.
- Conservation of angular momentum. Gyroscopes and precession.

### Unit 6: Simple Harmonic Motion and Gravitation
- Simple harmonic oscillators: $\ddot{x} + \omega_0^2 x = 0$.
- Mass-spring systems and simple/physical pendulums.
- Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation: $F_g = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$.
- Kepler's three laws of planetary motion, escape velocity, and orbital mechanics.
