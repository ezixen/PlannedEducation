# Curriculum Standards — Singapore MOE (Primary & Secondary)

## Source
- **URL**: https://www.moe.gov.sg/education/syllabuses
- **Status**: Primary reference for test module creation
- **Rationale**: Singapore consistently ranks #1 in PISA/TIMSS for math and science. Clear grade-by-grade progression. Separate sciences from upper secondary.

## Grade Structure (Singapore System)

| Level | Grades | Age | Science Subjects |
|-------|--------|-----|------------------|
| Lower Primary | P1-P3 | 7-9 | Science (combined) |
| Upper Primary | P4-P6 | 10-12 | Science (combined) |
| Lower Secondary | Sec 1-3 | 13-15 | Science (combined) → separate at Sec 3 |
| Upper Secondary | Sec 4-5 / JC 1-2 | 16-18 | Physics, Chemistry, Biology (separate) |

## Grade 1 (Age 7) — Lower Primary

### Science (Combined)
**Topics:**
- Living things: Plants, animals, humans
- Materials: Properties of materials (hard/soft, rough/smooth, float/sink)
- Plants and animals: Basic needs, parts of plants
- Weather: Sun, rain, wind, clouds
- Light and dark: Sources of light, shadows

### English Language
**Topics:**
- Phonics: Letter sounds, blending, segmenting
- Simple sentences: Subject-verb-object structure
- Vocabulary: Common nouns, verbs, adjectives
- Reading comprehension: Short passages, picture-based questions

## Grade 2 (Age 8) — Lower Primary

### Science (Combined)
**Topics:**
- Living things: Life cycles (butterfly, frog, chicken)
- Materials: Changes in materials (heating, cooling, mixing)
- Plants and animals: Habitats, food chains
- Weather: Seasons, water cycle basics
- Light and sound: Sources, how they travel

### English Language
**Topics:**
- Phonics: Digraphs, trigraphs, silent letters
- Grammar: Nouns, verbs, adjectives, simple tenses
- Composition: Picture-based writing, simple stories
- Comprehension: Short stories, answering questions

## Grade 3 (Age 9) — Lower Primary

### Science (Combined)
**Topics:**
- Living things: Classification of living things
- Materials: Magnets, forces (push, pull)
- Plants and animals: Plant parts and functions, animal groups
- Weather: Water cycle, weather instruments
- Light and sound: Reflection, pitch, volume

### English Language
**Topics:**
- Grammar: Tenses (present, past, future), prepositions, conjunctions
- Composition: Story writing, descriptive writing
- Comprehension: Longer passages, inference questions
- Vocabulary: Synonyms, antonyms, homophones

## Grade 4 (Age 10) — Upper Primary

### Science (Combined)
**Topics:**
- Cycles: Life cycles, matter cycles, water cycle
- Systems: Plant systems, human systems (digestive, respiratory)
- Energy: Forms of energy, energy conversion
- Interactions: Forces, magnets, food webs

### English Language
**Topics:**
- Grammar: Complex sentences, reported speech, conditionals
- Composition: Narrative writing, expository writing
- Comprehension: Non-fiction passages, critical thinking
- Vocabulary: Idioms, phrasal verbs, context clues

## Grade 5 (Age 11) — Upper Primary

### Science (Combined)
**Topics:**
- Cycles: Reproduction in plants, reproduction in humans
- Systems: Electrical systems, circulatory system
- Energy: Photosynthesis, respiration, energy sources
- Interactions: Environment, adaptations, ecosystems

### English Language
**Topics:**
- Grammar: Passive voice, modal verbs, relative clauses
- Composition: Argumentative writing, situational writing
- Comprehension: Visual texts, data interpretation
- Vocabulary: Collocations, word families

## Grade 6 (Age 12) — Upper Primary (PSLE Preparation)

### Science (Combined)
**Topics:**
- All P3-P5 topics reviewed at deeper level
- Energy: Energy conversion, efficiency
- Interactions: Forces, pressure, environmental impact
- Review: PSLE-style questions, exam techniques

### English Language
**Topics:**
- All P3-P5 topics reviewed at deeper level
- Composition: All text types (narrative, descriptive, expository, argumentative)
- Comprehension: All text types, synthesis and transformation
- Vocabulary: Advanced vocabulary, contextual usage

## Grade 7 (Age 13) — Lower Secondary

### Science (Combined)
**Topics:**
- Scientific method: Hypothesis, variables, fair testing
- Cells: Cell structure, cell functions, microscopy
- Forces: Types of forces, effects of forces, pressure
- Energy: Forms, conversion, conservation, sources
- Matter: Particulate nature, states of matter, changes of state
- Chemical reactions: Chemical vs physical changes, common reactions

### English Language
**Topics:**
- Grammar: Advanced tenses, conditionals, reported speech
- Composition: Argumentative, discursive, reflective writing
- Comprehension: Unseen passages, summary writing
- Oral: Reading aloud, stimulus-based conversation

## Grade 8 (Age 14) — Lower Secondary

### Science (Combined)
**Topics:**
- Cells: Cell division, transport in cells
- Forces: Pressure, moments, energy sources
- Energy: Work, power, efficiency, renewable energy
- Matter: Atomic structure, elements, compounds, mixtures
- Chemical reactions: Acids and bases, salts, redox reactions

### English Language
**Topics:**
- Grammar: Advanced grammar structures
- Composition: All text types at secondary level
- Comprehension: Narrative and non-narrative texts
- Oral: Presentation skills, group discussion

## Grade 9 (Age 15) — Lower Secondary

### Science (Combined)
**Topics:**
- Cells: Transport in humans, transport in plants
- Forces: Turning effects, pressure in fluids
- Energy: Thermal energy, kinetic theory, gas laws
- Matter: Periodic table, chemical bonding
- Chemical reactions: Rate of reaction, energy changes

### English Language
**Topics:**
- Grammar: Mastery of all structures
- Composition: Exam-style writing (O-Level preparation)
- Comprehension: Exam-style comprehension
- Oral: Exam-style oral communication

## Grade 10 (Age 16) — Upper Secondary

### Physics
**Topics:**
- Physical quantities, units, measurement
- Kinematics: Speed, velocity, acceleration
- Dynamics: Newton's laws, friction, momentum
- Mass, weight, density
- Turning effects of forces
- Pressure
- Energy, work, power
- Kinetic model of matter
- Transfer of thermal energy
- Temperature
- Thermal properties of matter

### Chemistry
**Topics:**
- Experimental chemistry: Apparatus, measurements, purification
- Atomic structure: Protons, neutrons, electrons, isotopes
- Structure and bonding: Ionic, covalent, metallic bonding
- Chemical reactions: Types, equations, balancing
- Stoichiometry: Mole concept, molar mass, concentration
- Acids and bases: Properties, reactions, pH
- Salts: Preparation, properties
- Periodic table: Groups, periods, trends

### Biology
**Topics:**
- Cell structure and organisation
- Movement of substances: Diffusion, osmosis, active transport
- Biological molecules: Carbohydrates, proteins, fats
- Nutrition: Human nutrition, plant nutrition
- Transport: Transport in humans, transport in plants
- Respiration: Aerobic, anaerobic
- Coordination and response: Nervous system, hormones
- Reproduction: Asexual, sexual, human reproduction

### English Language
**Topics:**
- Composition: Argumentative, expository, narrative
- Comprehension: Narrative and non-narrative texts
- Oral: Reading aloud, stimulus-based conversation
- Listening: Note-taking, summary

## Grade 11 (Age 17) — Upper Secondary

### Physics
**Topics:**
- Waves: General wave properties, light, sound
- Static electricity: Charges, electric fields
- Current electricity: Current, voltage, resistance, circuits
- D.C. circuits: Series, parallel, potential dividers
- Practical electricity: Domestic wiring, safety
- Magnetism: Magnetic fields, electromagnetism
- Electromagnetic induction: Faraday's law, Lenz's law
- Radioactivity: Atomic model, half-life, safety

### Chemistry
**Topics:**
- Air: Composition, pollutants, greenhouse effect
- Organic chemistry: Alkanes, alkenes, alcohols, carboxylic acids, polymers
- Energy changes: Exothermic, endothermic, bond energy
- Reaction rates: Factors, collision theory
- Reversible reactions: Equilibrium, Le Chatelier's principle
- Redox: Oxidation, reduction, reactivity series
- Extraction of metals: Reactivity series, electrolysis

### Biology
**Topics:**
- Transport in humans: Circulatory system, lymphatic system
- Transport in plants: Xylem, phloem, transpiration
- Respiration: Aerobic, anaerobic, energy release
- Coordination and response: Nervous system, endocrine system
- Reproduction: Human reproduction, sexual reproduction in plants
- Inheritance: Genetics, DNA, variation, natural selection
- Ecology: Ecosystems, food webs, pollution, conservation

### English Language
**Topics:**
- Composition: Advanced argumentative, discursive
- Comprehension: Advanced unseen passages
- Oral: Advanced presentation, discussion
- Listening: Advanced note-taking

## Grade 12 (Age 18) — Pre-University / JC

### Physics
**Topics:**
- Measurement: SI units, vectors, scalars
- Kinematics: Graphs, equations of motion
- Dynamics: Newton's laws, momentum, impulse
- Forces: Equilibrium, moments, pressure
- Work, energy, power: Conservation of energy
- Motion in a circle: Centripetal force
- Gravitational fields: Newton's law of gravitation
- Temperature: Thermal equilibrium, thermometers
- Ideal gases: Gas laws, kinetic theory
- First law of thermodynamics
- Oscillations: Simple harmonic motion, resonance
- Waves: Superposition, interference, diffraction
- Electric fields: Coulomb's law, electric potential
- Current of electricity: Drift velocity, EMF, internal resistance
- D.C. circuits: Kirchhoff's laws, potential dividers
- Electromagnetism: Magnetic fields, force on current-carrying conductor
- Electromagnetic induction: Faraday's law, Lenz's law, generators
- Alternating currents: RMS values, transformers
- Quantum physics: Photoelectric effect, atomic spectra, wave-particle duality
- Nuclear physics: Radioactive decay, nuclear reactions, mass-energy equivalence

### Chemistry
**Topics:**
- Atomic structure: Subatomic particles, isotopes, mass spectrometry
- Chemical bonding: Ionic, covalent, metallic, intermolecular forces
- The gaseous state: Ideal gas equation, real gases
- Chemical energetics: Enthalpy changes, Hess's law, bond energies
- Electrochemistry: Redox, electrode potentials, electrolysis
- Equilibria: Chemical equilibrium, equilibrium constants
- Reaction kinetics: Rate equations, activation energy, catalysts
- Periodicity: Periodic trends, Group 2, Group 17
- Group 2: Alkaline earth metals
- Group 17: Halogens
- Nitrogen and sulfur: Compounds, environmental impact
- Organic chemistry: Isomerism, alkanes, alkenes, arenes, halogenoalkanes, alcohols, phenols, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, derivatives, nitrogen compounds, polymerisation
- Organic synthesis: Multi-step synthesis, analytical techniques

### Biology
**Topics:**
- Cell and ultrastructure: Prokaryotic, eukaryotic, organelles
- Biological molecules: Carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, nucleic acids, water
- Enzymes: Structure, function, factors affecting activity
- Cell membranes and transport: Fluid mosaic model, transport mechanisms
- Cell cycle and division: Mitosis, meiosis, cancer
- Nucleic acids and protein synthesis: DNA replication, transcription, translation
- Genetic variation: Mutation, recombination, independent assortment
- Energy and respiration: Glycolysis, Krebs cycle, oxidative phosphorylation
- Photosynthesis: Light-dependent, light-independent reactions
- Homeostasis: Temperature, blood glucose, water potential
- Coordination: Nervous system, endocrine system
- Inherited change: Mendelian genetics, linkage, epistasis
- Selection and evolution: Natural selection, speciation, evidence
- Biodiversity: Classification, conservation
- Genetic technology: Recombinant DNA, PCR, gel electrophoresis

### English Language
**Topics:**
- Composition: Advanced argumentative, critical response
- Comprehension: Complex unseen passages, critical analysis
- Oral: Advanced presentation, debate
- Listening: Advanced comprehension, note-taking
