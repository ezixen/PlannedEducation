# PlannedEducation — Student Guide 🎒

> Student guide for registering, taking exams securely, keeping recovery codes, and recovering access.

---

## 1. Registration & The 5 Recovery Codes

### Creating Your Account
1. Open the platform URL and click **Register**.
2. Select **Student**, type your school email, chosen username, full name, and password.
3. **CRITICAL: The 5 Recovery Codes Modal**:
   - As soon as you register, you will see a screen with **5 one-time recovery codes**.
   - **Print them or write them down immediately in your notebook!**
   - Each code can be used once to instantly reset your password all by yourself, without needing anyone's permission or waiting for emails.

---

## 2. Taking Exams with Safe Exam Browser (SEB)

### Before the Exam
1. Verify that **Safe Exam Browser (SEB)** is installed on your testing device (provided by your school or downloaded from the official SEB website).
2. Ensure you have your school credentials ready.

### Starting Your Exam
1. Log in to PlannedEducation and click on your assigned exam under **My Exams**.
2. Click **Launch Exam in SEB** or download the `.seb` exam configuration file.
3. SEB will launch and lock your workstation into full-screen exam mode.
4. Shortcuts like Alt+Tab, Windows Key, and background browser tabs are blocked during the test.

### Offline Resilience & Auto-Save
- PlannedEducation saves your answers automatically as you type every few seconds.
- If your school Wi-Fi or network disconnects temporarily during an exam, **do not panic**: the exam continues running offline locally. When network connection resumes, answers sync automatically.
- Once you reach the end of the test, click **Submit Exam**.

## 3. Password Recovery: 3 Easy Ways

If you forget your password, you have 3 ways to regain access:

### Method 1: Self-Reset with Your Recovery Codes (Fastest!)
1. On the login screen, click **Forgot Password?**.
2. Enter your email and one of your **5 one-time recovery codes**.
3. Choose a new password and sign in immediately. No teacher or parent approval needed!

### Method 2: Emergency Classroom Unlock by Your Teacher (Right Before an Exam!)
If you are at school right before an exam and forgot your credentials:
1. Raise your hand and tell your teacher: *"I can't log in."*
2. Your teacher clicks the yellow **"⚡ Reset Pwd for Test"** button next to your name in their roster.
3. Go to the login screen (`/login`), type your email, and press enter.
4. The system detects your teacher's unlock, displays a green message, and asks you to type your new password right there.
5. Enter your new password, click **Set Password and Sign In**, and start your exam right away!

### Method 3: In-App Password Reset Request
If you ran out of recovery codes and are not in class:
1. Click **Forgot Password?** → **Request Supervisor Reset**.
2. Select your linked **Teacher** or **Parent**.
3. Your supervisor receives a notification on their dashboard and can generate a temporary reset code for you.

## 4. Reviewing Your Grades & Feedback

1. Once your teacher has reviewed and released the exam results, navigate to **My Results**.
2. Inspect your numerical score, letter grade, and line-by-line teacher comments.
3. Review question-by-question explanations to help prepare for upcoming topics.
