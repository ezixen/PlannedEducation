# PlannedEducation — Parent Guide 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

> Parent guide for monitoring academic progress, linking student accounts, and managing child account recovery.

---

## 1. Registration & Account Linking

### Creating a Parent Account
1. Open the platform URL and click **Register**.
2. Select **Parent**, fill in your details, and set a password.
3. Save your **5 one-time recovery codes** displayed upon registration.

### Linking to Your Child's Account
1. Once logged in, navigate to **My Children**.
2. Click **Link Student Account**.
3. Enter your child's student identifier or linkage code provided by their teacher or school.
4. Once verified, your child's academic record is securely connected to your portal.

---

## 2. Viewing Grades, Progress & Teacher Feedback

### Progress Dashboard
- View upcoming exams, completed assignments, and recent grading scores.
- Review detailed teacher feedback and rubric breakdowns for each question.
- Track mastery across curriculum subjects over the school term.

### Privacy & FERPA Compliance
- PlannedEducation strictly complies with FERPA and GDPR student privacy rules.
- Your child's exam answers, webcam streams (if proctored), and academic logs are strictly private to you, your child, and authorized school teachers.
- No student records or personal data are ever sold, transmitted to third-party advertisers, or shared with commercial entities.

---

## 3. Helping Your Child Recover Their Password

If your child forgets their password and has exhausted their 5 one-time recovery codes:
1. Your child can select you as their supervisor from the **Forgot Password?** screen.
2. A red badge will appear on your **Notification Bell** at the top of your dashboard.
3. Open the notification and click **Approve Reset** to generate a secure temporary password.
4. Give the temporary password to your child so they can sign in and set their new password.
